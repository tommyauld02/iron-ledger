Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

MNTL D&T keeps your log on your phone. We have no account system, no servers that store your log, no ads and no tracking. The only things that leave your phone are the food descriptions you type into Ask an agent, and the app's own update downloads.

MNTL D&T is made by Thomas Alexander Auld ("we", "us").

What stays on your phone

Everything you log: food, macros, water, body weight, workouts, sets, cardio, routines, notes, the daily checklist and your settings. It is stored on your device only, in the app's own storage on iPhone or your browser's storage on the web version. We cannot see it, and we keep no copy. Your phone's own backups (iCloud Backup, or a backup to a computer) may include it, as they do for any app.

  • Camera: barcode scanning and label reading happen on your phone. Pictures are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Grocery brands: the barcode data (from the US Department of Agriculture) is downloaded to your phone once and looked up there.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • Ask an agent. Only after you give permission in the app (you can turn it off again in Settings). When you ask it about a food, the words you typed (for example "chicken burrito bowl") and your country setting are sent to our relay, a small server run on Cloudflare, which passes them to an AI provider to estimate the nutrition. Today that provider is Google (Gemini). If we add another, we will name it here and ask your permission again before using it. Your name, your log and anything else on your phone are not sent. Your phone's IP address is seen by Cloudflare, as with any internet request, and is used only to limit how many questions one phone can send per minute. We use Google's free Gemini service, which may keep the text, use it to improve Google's products, and have people at Google review it, so do not type personal information into Ask an agent.
  • App updates. The app downloads its own updates from our website, hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub sees a normal web request, including your IP address.
  • Things you choose to send. Send a copy (your backup) and routine sharing codes go only where you send them, using your phone's share sheet.
  • Apple Health (when the feature is added): only with your permission, written to Health on your phone, never sent to us, and never used for advertising.

What we don't do

No ads. No analytics or tracking. No selling or sharing of personal information. No accounts and no sign-in.

Your choices

  • You can decline or withdraw the Ask an agent permission at any time and keep using the rest of the app fully without Ask an agent.
  • To delete your data, delete the app (or clear the site's data on the web version). Because we store no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Make a backup first with Send a copy if you want to keep it.

Children

MNTL D&T is for adults 18 and older, and the app asks you to confirm your age before use. It stores only your yes/no answer on your phone, never a birth date or ID. If you are under 18, please don't use it. If you believe someone under 18 has used Ask an agent or contacted us, email mntldntsupport@gmail.com and we will delete any message from them we hold.

California residents

We do not sell or share personal information. The only personal information involved is the food text and IP address described above, used only to answer your request.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date and say so in the app's What's new. Questions: mntldntsupport@gmail.com.
