Where is my log kept?

On your phone only. There is no account and no copy on a server, so we cannot see or restore it for you. That is also why backups matter.

How do I back up, or move to a new phone?

  1. In the app, open Backup & restore. Tap Copy, or Send a copy to save it as a file (named like mntl-dt-2026-10-27.json), and keep it somewhere safe, such as Notes, Files, iCloud Drive or an email to yourself.
  2. On the new phone, install MNTL D&T, open Backup & restore, paste the backup text into the box and tap Restore from text.

Restoring adds the backup to what is already on the phone rather than wiping it: for each day, whichever copy was changed most recently is kept.

The dot next to Backup reminds you when it's been a while.

I used the web version on my home screen. How do I move to the App Store app?

The two keep separate logs. In the web version, open Backup & restore and tap Copy (or Send a copy). Then in the App Store app, open Backup & restore, paste the text into the box and tap Restore from text.

Ask an agent isn't answering

  • It needs an internet connection; everything else in the app works offline.
  • If it says the food finder is busy, its free daily allowance has run out. It resets within a day.
  • Typing the food differently ("grilled chicken breast, 6 oz") often helps.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Because nothing is stored on our side, that removes everything. Make a backup first if you might want it back.

Why does it ask if I'm 18?

MNTL D&T is for adults. The AI service behind Ask an agent only allows adult users, so the app asks once and remembers only your yes or no, on your phone.

Is this medical advice?

No. MNTL D&T is a log. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before changing your diet or training. See the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
